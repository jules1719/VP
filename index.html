What it does

Measure · Remind · Learn

Measure

A smart scale base weighs your bottle and recognises drinking activity.

Remind

A soft RGB light reminds you when it is time to drink, even without Wi-Fi.

Learn

A simple dashboard shows your daily progress and weekly trend.

How it works

Three calm steps

  1. Step 1

    Slide in, set down

    Slide your bottle into the flexible silicone sleeve and place it on the HydroSip base. No new bottle needed.

  2. Step 2

    It understands what happened

    HydroSip notices what really happened: a motion sensor ignores lifting and tilting, the scale compares stable weight readings, and the system tells a sip from a refill.

  3. Step 3

    A gentle glow

    A gentle glow reminds you to drink; your history syncs to your dashboard when a connection is available.

An honest note: Weight is an indirect measure. Spilling or pouring out can look like drinking, so HydroSip shows estimates, not medical data.

FAQ

Good questions

Does it fit my bottle?

HydroSip is designed for common bottle sizes; two sleeve sizes are currently being tested.

Does it work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Reminders run locally on the device. Your history syncs when a connection is available.

Is it a medical device?

No. HydroSip is a hydration reminder and shows estimates.

How is it cleaned?

Simply remove the bottle and clean it as usual. The electronics stay dry in the base.
